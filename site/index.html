Pythagorean chart

Each letter takes the number of its column, from 1 to 9. The total is reduced by adding its digits until one digit is left. The master numbers 11, 22 and 33 are not reduced.

Chaldean chart

Letters are grouped by sound and take values from 1 to 8. No letter has the value 9. The total before reducing is called the compound number.
